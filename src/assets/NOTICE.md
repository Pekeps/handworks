# Third-party assets

`left.glb` and `right.glb` are the `generic-hand` models from
[`@webxr-input-profiles/assets`](https://github.com/immersive-web/webxr-input-profiles)
v1.0.20, © the Immersive Web Community Group contributors, used under the
MIT license. The models are rigged with the 25-joint skeleton defined by the
[WebXR Hand Input specification](https://www.w3.org/TR/webxr-hand-input-1/).
